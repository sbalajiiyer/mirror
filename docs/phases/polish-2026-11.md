# Product polish (November 2026): demo script

**Built:**
- **Multi-language rater form**: English, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese.
  - Raters pick a language at the top of the form. The default comes from the browser, and the choice is
    remembered on that device (`?lang=es` also works).
  - Switching language keeps the rater's place and answers.
  - Built-in "Mirror Core 360" statements and competency names have reviewed translations
    (`src/lib/i18n/core-360.ts`).
  - Custom statements are machine-translated once per language (free model), cached on the question, and shown
    with a short note.
  - The subject can review and correct them under **Translations for raters** on the 360's page, or translate
    everything up front.
  - The subject's invitation message is shown as written. The self-assessment and the rest of the app stay in
    English.
  - EU-region organizations: machine translation follows the same AI residency rule (it falls back to English).
- **AI coach replies stream**:
  - Replies appear as they're written: Groq/OpenAI and Anthropic streaming, with the sample reply streamed in demo
    mode.
  - Citations arrive at the end, and both messages are saved once the reply is complete.
  - If you leave mid-reply, it is still saved.
  - Screen readers get the finished reply, not every word.
- **Dark mode switch** in the header: match device → light → dark. It's remembered on the device and applied
  before the page paints (no flash). Accessibility checks pass in dark mode.
- **Coach onboarding**:
  - People who become coaches by accepting a client's invitation, or who pick **"Are you a coach?"** during
    sign-up, get a short coach setup: name, practice name, who you coach, website.
  - They skip the self-360 questions and land in the coach workspace.
- **AI comment themes**:
  - **Group comments with AI** on a report re-groups every written comment by meaning (any language) into 3–7
    named themes, each with a one-line summary and tone.
  - Switch between "By meaning (AI)" and "By keyword".
  - Only the comment text is sent, with emails, phone numbers and links scrubbed. It's cleared when retention
    deletes the comments.

## Try it
1. **Alex:** open the "Leadership check-in" 360 and add a rater. Open their link from `/dev/outbox` and switch the
   language.
2. Click the monitor/sun/moon icon in the header to change theme.
3. On the baseline "Pre-promotion 360" report, click **Group comments with AI**.
4. **AI coach:** ask a question and watch the reply appear.
5. Sign up with a new email and choose **Set up your coaching practice instead**.
